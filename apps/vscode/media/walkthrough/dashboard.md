## The dashboard

The dashboard opens in an editor tab, from the status bar item or the sidebar, and follows your colour theme.
It is in the panel too, in the **Usage dashboard** tab beside the terminal, under a strip that keeps Claude Code's five-hour and weekly windows, Copilot CLI's allowance and the latest session in this project in sight while you work.

- **Overview**: today, this week and this month, cost per day and plan limits.
- **Sessions**, **Projects** and **Models**: where the usage goes.
- **Limits**: every agent's plan windows, their pace and recent five-hour windows.
- **Settings**: data folders, plan limit sources, your history and time zone.

Claude Code's cost is an API-equivalent estimate at Anthropic's list prices, not what your plan charges.
Copilot CLI's cost is what Copilot CLI recorded, at $0.01 an AI credit.
A figure that adds both says it includes an estimate.
